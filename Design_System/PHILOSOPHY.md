# ar.dev design philosophy

The site helps a general visitor understand Devang's work and lets a technical reader inspect its current state. Architecture, research and AI share one personal narrative. The first release contains three project stories, two explicitly labeled AI reinterpretations, and a dated update log.

This replaces the prior ET Book / 3D bookshelf direction for this implementation. Keep earlier studies intact. Apple-style product storytelling governs the hierarchy: generous space, clear sans-serif type, purposeful imagery, restrained controls. Sufi informs the project index and release-log pattern; no copy, branding, statistics or artwork is borrowed.

Use neutral light and dark surfaces with a restrained green accent. The Reimagined section is the cinematic interlude. Natural scrolling, one brief nonessential hero reveal, no autoplay, no scroll hijacking, no WebGL. Images, copy and controls remain available with reduced motion. The user also has a local reduced-motion preference, which cannot override an operating-system request to reduce motion.

Original academic renders and field photographs are evidence of the original work. Generated studies are marked wherever presented. Never imply generated terrain is a real project location or that generated landscape changes have demonstrated environmental performance. Never imply a code prototype is a published app.

The site is static, dependency-free, local-asset-only and subpath-compatible. Source paths, prompts and raw material stay outside `site/`. Public output contains only reviewed presentation content and the existing professional contact and reflection links.
