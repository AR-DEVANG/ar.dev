Research / Edward Guiliano Global Fellowship

Better materials.
Bigger questions.

A field study into what it takes for ultra-high-performance concrete to move from technical promise to everyday infrastructure.

Explore the research Completed · 2026

Journey in Motion

My Edward Guiliano Global Fellowship project at New York Institute of Technology examined UHPC and climate-resilient infrastructure through field research in Berlin, Paris, Nagpur, and Udaipur, from February to March 2026.

My contribution

I compared material adoption through site observations, manufacturing-standards research, and construction economics. The project asks how financing and procurement influence whether a durable material gets used.

The record

The completed project is documented in research notes, a project summary, and a reflection film.

Architecture / Academic work

A pause in
the journey.

Way Side Amenities: an architectural study of the spaces between destinations, bringing places to stop, rest, and gather into one setting.

Explore the project Archived

The starting point

My B.Arch thesis, “Way Side Amenities,” submitted to Priyadarshini Institute of Architecture and Design Studies, Nagpur. The archive includes the report, working material, and architectural visualizations.

My contribution

The academic design explores a roadside precinct through pavilion forms, circulation, and shared outdoor spaces. These are design visualizations, not photographs of a completed building.

What comes next

The original design now provides a starting point for two new visual studies: a planted arrival landscape and an evening hospitality setting.
